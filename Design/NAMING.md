# Look Far

The selected name is **Look Far**. The subtitle is **Eye Strain Relief & Breaks**. The name describes the distance-looking habit directly; the subtitle makes the app’s purpose clear. This wording does not change the product’s medical limits: breaks are not a treatment for myopia or retinal disease.

Use **Look Far** inside the app and **LookFar** in Swift types, project files, and the Xcode scheme. Apple rejected the exact store title as already used; the user selected **Look Far: Eye Strain Relief**, now saved in App Store Connect. The app bundle is `dev.local.lookfar`; its App Group is `group.dev.local.lookfar`. Subscription products are `dev.lookfar.plus.annual` and `dev.lookfar.plus.monthly`.

Brand text is centralized in `App/Theme.swift`. The app, extensions, subscription screens, project configuration, and documentation use the chosen name. The earlier Stillfar bundle’s local records are not automatically migrated to the new app identity.

## Naming research

These observations are retained from the September 19, 2026 review. They are not trademark, domain, or App Store availability clearance.

| Candidate | Relevant existing uses | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Look Far / Lookfar** | [LookFar Labs](https://www.lookfar.com/) builds software and mobile apps. [The LookMobile’s LookFar Project](https://www.thelookmobile.com/about) is an eyewear donation program. | Chosen for its clear, positive action. Existing use in software and an adjacent vision category still needs review before public launch. |
| **Unfocus** | An existing [Unfocus desktop break-reminder app](https://github.com/abhiksark/unfocus) asks users to look at something far away. [Unfocus: Movie Poster Trivia](https://apps.apple.com/tt/app/unfocus-movie-poster-trivia/id6763316142) is also an iOS app. | Rejected because the exact name already identifies an eye-break product and may suggest blurred vision. |

Earlier alternatives were Stillfar, VistaPause, Farquiet, and Calm Vista. Those names are no longer active candidates.

## Visual direction

Design source: `selected-reference.png`, the third displayed concept chosen by the user. The source preserves the earlier concept’s lettering; the implemented app uses Look Far. Landscape and icon assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, saved in the asset catalog, and inspected locally. The landscape uses a dark forest lake, layered hills, a small warm moon, and left-side pines without UI or lettering. The icon uses a minimal horizon arc and distant sun on a forest background.
